Scenic River Float

Relax on this half-day trip along calm, crystal-clear waters. Perfect for families and beginners. Includes a riverside picnic and plenty of photo opportunities.

White Water Rush

Experience the thrill of Class III and IV rapids on this action-packed full-day adventure. Safety gear and lunch included. Ideal for thrill-seekers.

Multi-Day Expedition

Spend three days exploring the wildest parts of the river. Camp under the stars, enjoy campfire meals, and navigate both calm and challenging sections of the river.
